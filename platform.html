Continuous High-Touch Remote Monitoring

Our FDA-cleared wearable and platform provide constant, passive biometric monitoring with ICU-level accuracy—backed by AI-enabled adverse event detection.

FDA-Cleared Wearable Device

Our proprietary upper-arm wearable continuously tracks critical vitals without patient burden or adherence challenges:

  • Respiratory rate
  • Heart rate and heart rate variability
  • Skin temperature
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)
  • Activity levels and movement

The device delivers continuous data with ICU-level accuracy, enabling our AI algorithms to detect early signs of deterioration and adverse events like cytokine release syndrome.

AI-Enabled Adverse Event Detection

Our machine learning algorithms analyze continuous biometric data to detect emerging adverse events earlier than standard of care—enabling proactive intervention.

🔥 CRS Detection

Our algorithms have been shown to detect cytokine release syndrome significantly earlier than clinical observation alone, analyzing patterns in temperature, heart rate, and respiratory rate to identify the early inflammatory cascade.

🧠 ICANS Monitoring

Virtual neurotoxicity assessments conducted by our clinical team, computing ICE scores remotely and triggering immediate escalation for grade 2+ events.

🦠 Sepsis & Infection

Early warning system for infectious complications using vital sign trend analysis and symptom correlation.

📉 General Deterioration

Configurable alarms and clinical pathways for detecting any unexpected change in patient status requiring intervention.

Biomarker & AI Model Development

Our proprietary dataset—the largest worldwide biometric data asset for community delivery of advanced therapies—powers the development of next-generation predictive models and digital biomarkers.

  • Predictive Biomarkers: Developing AI-enabled biomarkers for early detection of CRS, ICANS, and other adverse events—often hours before clinical signs appear
  • SaMD Pipeline: FDA Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) tools in development for clinical decision support
