Research Focus Areas

Our research program advances the understanding of safety, efficacy, and best practices for ambulatory delivery of advanced therapies.

🔥

CRS Detection & Management

Developing and validating AI algorithms for early cytokine release syndrome detection. Understanding the physiological signatures that precede clinical manifestation.

🧠

ICANS Assessment

Validating virtual neurotoxicity assessment protocols. Developing remote ICE scoring methodologies and escalation criteria.

🏠

Ambulatory Safety

Building the evidence base for outpatient CAR-T and bispecific delivery. Defining eligibility criteria and monitoring protocols.

📊

Outcomes Research

Characterizing real-world outcomes including response rates, adverse events, hospitalizations, and quality of life measures.

💰

Health Economics

Quantifying cost savings from ambulatory delivery. Supporting value-based contracting with robust economic evidence.

🧬

Digital Biomarkers

Developing novel physiological signatures for early detection of adverse events. Training AI models on continuous biometric data.

Outpatient CAR-T Delivery at Scale

Blood 2024 • Sarah Cannon, Current Health

Demonstrates that 90%+ of CAR-T therapies can be safely delivered in outpatient settings with continuous remote monitoring. Establishes the safety profile for multisite ambulatory programs.

doi.org/10.1182/blood-2024-211566 →
📄

Early CRS Detection via Continuous Monitoring

JCO Oncology Practice 2025 • Multiple Institutions

Validates that continuous wearable monitoring can detect CRS earlier than standard clinical observation, enabling proactive intervention and reduced hospitalizations.

doi.org/10.1200/OP-25-00062 →
📄

OUTREACH Trial: Outpatient CAR-T Feasibility

Blood 2023 • Multicenter

70% outpatient infusion rate with 25% of patients never requiring hospitalization. Demonstrates strong response rates and safety outcomes in ambulatory settings.

doi.org/10.1182/blood-2023-181936 →

Program Quality Metrics

We work with partners to track standardized quality metrics, enabling continuous improvement and benchmarking.

📊 Shift Rate

% of CAR-T/bispecific episodes successfully delivered in outpatient settings

🏥 Hospitalization Rate

% of patients requiring unplanned hospitalization during monitoring period

🚑 ED Visit Rate

% of patients with emergency department visits during episode

🔥 CRS Rates

Overall CRS rate and distribution by grade (1, 2, 3+)

🧠 ICANS Rate

Incidence of immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome

✅ Adherence

Patient compliance with monitoring protocols and device wear
