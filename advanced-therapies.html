Why Advanced Therapies Need a New Delivery Model

The traditional model of extended inpatient stays for CAR-T and bispecific monitoring limits access, strains capacity, and adds unnecessary cost. With the right infrastructure, most patients can be safely monitored in outpatient or home settings.

🏨

Inpatient Bottleneck

Standard of care requires 1-3 weeks of inpatient monitoring, but most hospitals have limited beds. This caps how many patients can receive life-saving therapies.

💰

Unsustainable Costs

At $3,000+ per inpatient day, extended stays add $30-60K to already expensive treatments. Payers and health systems need a more sustainable model.

📍

Limited Access

Only ~150 certified centers can deliver CAR-T, mainly at urban AMCs. Patients travel hundreds of miles and stay weeks away from home. We can bring therapy closer to patients.

Our Proven Model for Safe Outpatient Delivery

We combine FDA-cleared continuous monitoring, AI-powered adverse event detection, and 24/7 clinical command center support to enable safe outpatient delivery of advanced therapies.

  • Continuous, passive health monitoring - removing patient and caregiver burden
  • AI-enabled early detection of CRS 4+ hours earlier than standard of care
  • Virtual ICANS assessments with ICE scoring by trained neurological RNs
  • 24/7 Clinical Command Center staffed by RNs licensed in all 50 states
  • Standardized escalation protocols, with in-home treatment and controlled escalation back to the treatment center
  • Longer term, low-touch symptom monitoring
