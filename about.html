A Decade of Shifting Care from Hospitals to Homes

Founded in Edinburgh in 2014, we've built the infrastructure that enables safe delivery of acute and complex care outside traditional hospital settings.

Our Mission

To be the industry's first AI-enabled clinical, technology, and financial infrastructure for making transformative advanced treatments accessible and sustainably affordable at scale.

Advanced therapies like CAR-T, bispecifics, and cell and gene therapies are transforming medicine—offering cures where none existed before. But these treatments are extremely costly, require extended inpatient stays, and are only available at a handful of urban academic medical centers.

We're building the infrastructure to change that. By enabling safe ambulatory and home-based delivery, we expand access, reduce costs, and improve the patient experience—all while maintaining the highest standards of safety and care quality.

🎯

Our Vision

A world where life-saving advanced therapies are accessible to every patient who needs them, regardless of where they live.

Our Journey

From a startup in Edinburgh to the leading infrastructure for advanced therapy delivery.

2014

Founded in Edinburgh

Current Health founded with a mission to deliver a safe, sustainable way to provide acute care at home. Built FDA-cleared technology and clinical infrastructure from the ground up.

2019

US Market Entry

Expanded to the United States, landing partnerships with leading health systems and global pharma companies. Established research-based brand credibility with peer-reviewed publications.

2021

Best Buy Acquisition

Acquired by Best Buy to accelerate hospital-at-home capabilities. Expanded team and scaled operations nationally.

2023

Advanced Therapies Launch

Partnered with Sarah Cannon to launch the largest multisite outpatient CAR-T program. Landed strategic partnerships with BMS and other global pharma.

2025

Management Re-Acquisition

Founders reacquired the company on highly advantageous terms. Right-sized operations, achieved sustainable unit economics, and refocused on advanced therapies as the primary growth driver.
