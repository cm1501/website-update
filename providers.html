Example CAR-T Patient Journey

From eligibility screening through step-down, Current Health provides continuous monitoring and clinical support.

DAY
-3

Eligibility & Kit Distribution

Patient identified as eligible for outpatient treatment based on safety criteria (caregiver presence, proximity to center, hotel availability). Current Health kit provided in clinic with training.

DAY
0

Infusion & Monitoring Begins

Patient receives CAR-T infusion in clinic, then returns home or to hotel. Current Health monitoring begins immediately with baseline vitals and ICANS assessment.

DAYS
0-15

Continuous Monitoring & Response

Patient remains at home/hotel with continuous biometric monitoring, daily ICANS assessments, and symptom surveys. AI algorithms detect early CRS signs. When grade 1 CRS is detected, home antipyretics may prevent escalation. Clinical Command Center available 24/7.

DAY
15+

Step-Down & Device Return

Patient graduates from intensive monitoring and steps down to lower-touch follow-up if needed. Kit returned via prepaid shipping for reprocessing. Outcomes data stored for research and quality improvement.

For Community Oncology Practices

The future of advanced treatments is in the community. We help you start and scale CAR-T and bispecific programs with turnkey infrastructure.

Why Community Delivery?

80% of the advanced therapy revenue opportunity is in community settings, not urban AMCs. Community practices can negotiate competitive case rates with commercial payers, generating net new revenue streams while serving patients closer to home.

  • More patients need treatment than AMC capacity allows
  • Literature demonstrates safe community delivery
  • Commercial case rates can deliver sustainable margins
  • Better patient experience closer to home
