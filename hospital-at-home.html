Why Hospital at Home

Hospital at Home is no longer an experiment— it's a proven model for delivering high-acuity care outside the four walls. For patients, it means faster recovery and fewer complications. For health systems, it's a strategic lever to reduce inpatient strain and shift appropriate care to a lower-cost, higher-margin setting.

💰

Top-Line Revenue Growth

Increase throughput and capture new revenue by expanding capacity beyond your physical beds. Treat more patients without capital investment in new facilities.

📊

Margin Improvement

Reduce ALOS, eliminate unnecessary inpatient stays, avoid readmissions and SNF transfers, and reduce total cost of care while maintaining quality.

😊

Better Patient & Caregiver Experience

Studies show H@H patients have better sleep and higher satisfaction, with minimal caregiver burden. For the right patients, hospital at home is simply better.

Regulatory Tailwinds

The Hospital at Home model has unprecedented regulatory support, with the CMS Acute Hospital Care at Home waiver extended through 2030.

🏛️

CMS AHCaH Waiver Extended

The Hospital Inpatient Services Modernization Act extends the Hospital at Home waiver through 2030, providing long-term regulatory certainty for your program.

💵

Full DRG Reimbursement

Hospital at Home patients qualify for the same DRG reimbursement as inpatient admissions, making the economics work for health systems.
